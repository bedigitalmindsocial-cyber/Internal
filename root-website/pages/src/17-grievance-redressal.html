Step 1

Write to our grievance desk

Tell us what went wrong and what you'd like us to do. Write from the email address registered with us, so we can find your account.

What to include
  • Your name and client code
  • The mobile number or email registered with us
  • What happened, with dates and any order or transaction numbers
  • What you'd like us to do
  • Screenshots or documents, if you have them
Email the grievance desk

Your email reaches {{reg.capital.name}}, which handles complaints about broking and depository services.

Never share your password or OTP, with us or anyone.

Step 3

Still not settled? Use SCORES or Smart ODR.

Both are run outside Aikyam. SCORES is SEBI's online complaints system. Smart ODR is the securities market's online dispute resolution portal: conciliation first, then arbitration if it isn't settled.

Mutual fund distribution

Is your complaint about mutual funds?

Aikyam Wealth distributes mutual funds on ROOT. Complaints about that service go to {{reg.wealth.name}}, not to the broking grievance desk.

Mutual funds, IPOs and bonds are not exchange traded products. Aikyam Wealth Private Limited acts only as a distributor (AMFI ARN {{reg.wealth.arn}}). Disputes arising from distribution activity do not have access to the exchange investor redressal forum or arbitration mechanism.

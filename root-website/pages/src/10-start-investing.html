How do I choose funds and start a SIP?

You choose the schemes, from regular plans across equity, debt and hybrid categories. Invest a fixed amount regularly (a SIP) or all at once. Each scheme sets its own minimum amount, shown in its scheme documents.

Help with choosing: what we can offer, to confirm. How SIP instalments are collected: to confirm.

I've done KYC before. Do I need to do it again?

KYC is one time exercise while dealing in securities markets. Once KYC is done through a SEBI registered intermediary (Broker, DP, Mutual Fund etc.), you need not undergo the same process again when you approach another intermediary.

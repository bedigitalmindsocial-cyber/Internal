Yes. You can invest through a SIP (a fixed amount invested at regular intervals) or in one go. The minimum amount is set by each scheme and shown in its scheme documents.

I have done KYC before. Do I need to do it again?

KYC is one time exercise while dealing in securities markets. Once KYC is done through a SEBI registered intermediary (Broker, DP, Mutual Fund etc.), you need not undergo the same process again when you approach another intermediary.

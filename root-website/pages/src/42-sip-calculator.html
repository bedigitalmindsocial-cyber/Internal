What does this estimate assume?

A calculator needs a few fixed rules. Here they are, and which ones you can change.

  • A return you chooseYou change this

    You set it, after the fund's own costs. It is not a past return or a forecast.

  • The same return every yearFixed here

    Real returns rise and fall, and can be negative in some years.

  • Money in at the start of each monthFixed here

    Each instalment starts earning from the month you pay it.

  • Before tax and inflationNot included

    Tax on gains and rising prices are not taken off the estimate.

How is the estimate worked out?

It's the future value of equal monthly instalments, each invested at the start of the month.

The formula

M = P × ({(1 + i)n − 1} ÷ i) × (1 + i)

M
Estimated value at the end
P
Your monthly instalment
i
Monthly rate: the yearly return ÷ 12 ÷ 100
n
Number of instalments: years × 12

Why the gap grows

Each month's gains are added to your money and earn gains of their own. At a steady return, that is why estimated gains rise faster in later years.

Worked example
Monthly instalment (P)₹5,000
Assumed return12% a year
Monthly rate (i)12 ÷ 12 ÷ 100 = 0.01
Instalments (n)10 × 12 = 120
(1 + i)n1.01120 = 3.30039
Growth factor({3.30039 − 1} ÷ 0.01) × 1.01 = 232.339
Estimated value (M)₹5,000 × 232.339 = ₹11,61,695
You invest₹5,000 × 120 = ₹6,00,000
Estimated gains₹11,61,695 − ₹6,00,000 = ₹5,61,695

What return should I put in?

ROOT doesn't suggest a rate or a fund. A scheme's past returns are in its factsheet, but past returns are not a forecast.

Try a lower and a higher rate to see how much the estimate depends on it.

Can I lose money in a SIP?

Yes. Mutual fund returns are not guaranteed. Your investment's value can go up or down, and can fall below what you put in.

Does the estimate include tax and inflation?

No. Tax on gains depends on the type of fund and how long you hold it, and tax rules can change. Current tax treatment: to confirm with compliance.

Prices also rise over time, so a future amount buys less than it would today.

How much do I need each month to reach a target?

Raise or lower the monthly amount until the estimated value reaches your target.

For a goal with a date, the retirement planner and the child education planner work back from the target.

What is the minimum SIP amount?

Each scheme sets its own minimum SIP amount, shown in its scheme documents.

Can I change, pause or stop my SIP later?

SIPs can be stopped. How and when on ROOT: change, pause and stop options to confirm.

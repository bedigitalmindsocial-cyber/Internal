Is this Aikyam's own PMS?

No. {{reg.wealth.name}} distributes PMS strategies from third-party portfolio managers registered with SEBI, empanelled for distribution. It does not manage your portfolio.

How Aikyam Wealth chooses the managers it distributes: to confirm.

How is PMS different from a mutual fund?

In a mutual fund, you own units of a fund shared with other investors. In PMS, the shares are held in your own account, and the portfolio manager manages them for you.

PMS has a SEBI minimum of {{pms.minimum}}. A mutual fund's minimum is set by each scheme. Both carry market risk.

Where is my money held, and do I need another account?

Your shares and other securities are held in a demat account in your name, and your agreement is with the portfolio manager.

Which company opens my account, and can I check it?

{{reg.capital.name}} opens and holds your trading and demat account. ROOT by Aikyam is its brand for investors. You can check its registration yourself on the SEBI website.

Where are my shares and money kept?

Your shares sit in your own demat account at NSDL, opened through {{reg.capital.name}} as a depository participant.

Pay only into the {{reg.capital.name}} bank accounts listed on the Client bank accounts page.

Does applying for an IPO cost anything?

Charges for applying: to confirm. Your bid amount is blocked in your bank account through your UPI mandate, and is debited only if you get an allotment.

I already trade with another broker. Can I open ROOT too?

Yes. Opening an account with Aikyam Capital does not need you to close an account elsewhere.

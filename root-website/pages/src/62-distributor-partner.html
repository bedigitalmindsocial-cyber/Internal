Can you apply?

You can partner with ROOT if you are registered to distribute mutual funds in India.

You're a registered distributor

  • NISM Series V-A certificate
  • An ARN (AMFI Registration Number), with your KYD (Know Your Distributor) checks complete
  • An EUIN for each person in your team who deals with investors

Individual distributors and distribution firms can apply. A firm applies with the firm's ARN. Any minimum experience, business size or location, to confirm.

Check Aikyam Wealth's ARN on AMFI's website

Always

  • Show your ARN and EUIN to clients and on every investment.
  • Tell clients they invest in regular plans, and what you earn from them.
  • Make sure each client's KYC is complete before they invest.
  • Explain each scheme's risk, using its riskometer and scheme documents.

Never

  • Promise returns, or say an investment cannot lose value.
  • Pass any part of your commission back to a client, or offer gifts to win business.
  • Take client money into your own account.
  • Switch a client's investments to earn more commission.
  • Use a title other than mutual fund distributor.

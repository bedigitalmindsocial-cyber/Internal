Which NAV will I get if I invest today?

You get the NAV of the business day on which your money reaches the fund house before the cut-off time SEBI sets.

Order times on ROOT: to confirm.

Growth or IDCW: what's the difference?

This page shows the Growth option: the fund reinvests what it earns, and that shows in the NAV.

With IDCW (income distribution cum capital withdrawal), the fund may pay out part of the NAV, which can include your own capital. The NAV falls by the amount paid out, and payouts are not guaranteed.

How are my gains taxed?

Gains on mutual fund units are taxed as capital gains when you sell. The rate depends on the type of fund and how long you held the units, and tax rules can change.

Which company holds my account, and where are my shares kept?

Aikyam Capital Private Limited, a SEBI-registered stock broker (INZ000321232), opens your trading and demat account. Your shares are held in your demat account at NSDL, opened through Aikyam Capital as a depository participant (IN-DP-854-2026 · NSDL DP IN305066).

Make payments only to the Aikyam Capital Private Limited bank accounts listed on the Client bank accounts page.

Mutual funds are distributed by Aikyam Wealth Private Limited (AMFI ARN to confirm). How your fund units are held: to confirm.

I only want mutual funds. Do I need a trading account?

No. Mutual funds on ROOT are distributed by Aikyam Wealth Private Limited. Start investing.

Whether one sign-up can open both: one onboarding for both, to confirm.

Can I trade F&O as soon as my account opens?

F&O is an optional segment. Before it is switched on, you share one proof of income. You can ask for it while you open your account or later, to confirm.

List to confirm with Aikyam compliance, for example: bank statement, latest ITR acknowledgement, salary slip or Form 16, net worth certificate, demat holdings statement

Are the mutual funds regular plans, and what does Aikyam Wealth earn?

Yes, regular plans. Aikyam Wealth Private Limited is paid a commission by the fund house, included in the plan's expense ratio.

Attention investor

  • Prevent unauthorised transactions in your account. Update your mobile numbers/email IDs with your stock brokers. Receive information of your transactions directly from Exchange on your mobile/email at the end of the day.
  • KYC is one time exercise while dealing in securities markets. Once KYC is done through a SEBI registered intermediary (Broker, DP, Mutual Fund etc.), you need not undergo the same process again when you approach another intermediary.
  • No need to issue cheques by investors while subscribing to IPO. Just write the bank account number and sign in the application form to authorize your bank to make payment in case of allotment.

Complaint about mutual fund distribution? It has its own route.

Step 1

Write to our grievance desk

Tell us what went wrong and what you'd like us to do. Write from the email address registered with us, so we can find your account.

What to include
  • Your name and client code
  • The mobile number or email registered with us
  • What happened, with dates and any order or transaction numbers
  • What you'd like us to do
  • Screenshots or documents, if you have them
Email the grievance desk

Your email reaches Aikyam Capital Private Limited, which handles complaints about broking and depository services.

Never share your password or OTP, with us or anyone.

Step 3

Still not settled? Use SCORES or Smart ODR.

Both are run outside Aikyam. SCORES is SEBI's online complaints system. Smart ODR is the securities market's online dispute resolution portal: conciliation first, then arbitration if it isn't settled.

Attention investor

  • Prevent unauthorised transactions in your account. Update your mobile numbers/email IDs with your stock brokers. Receive information of your transactions directly from Exchange on your mobile/email at the end of the day.
  • KYC is one time exercise while dealing in securities markets. Once KYC is done through a SEBI registered intermediary (Broker, DP, Mutual Fund etc.), you need not undergo the same process again when you approach another intermediary.
  • No need to issue cheques by investors while subscribing to IPO. Just write the bank account number and sign in the application form to authorize your bank to make payment in case of allotment.

How do I open an account with ROOT?

Enter your mobile number, verify your PAN, share your Aadhaar through DigiLocker, link your bank account and e-sign. The process is fully online.

Activation is subject to KYC verification and exchange approval.

What does it cost to open an account?

Opening an account is free. Brokerage and other charges are listed on the Pricing page.

Is ROOT registered with SEBI?

ROOT is offered by Aikyam Capital Private Limited, a SEBI-registered stock broker (INZ000321232) and a member of NSE, BSE, MCX and MSEI.

When will the ROOT app be available?

The iOS and Android apps are coming soon. You can open your account on the web today.

Can I invest in mutual funds through ROOT?

Yes. You can invest in plans across equity, debt and hybrid categories, as a SIP or a one-time investment.

How do I raise a complaint?

Write to grievances@aikyamcap.com. If you are not satisfied with the response, you can register your grievance on SEBI's SCORES portal or the Smart ODR portal.

Risk disclosures on derivatives

  • 9 out of 10 individual traders in equity Futures and Options Segment incurred net losses.
  • On an average, loss makers registered net trading loss close to Rs. 50,000.
  • Over and above net trading losses, loss makers expended an additional 28% of net trading losses as transaction costs.
  • Those making net trading profits incurred between 15% to 50% of such profit as transaction cost.

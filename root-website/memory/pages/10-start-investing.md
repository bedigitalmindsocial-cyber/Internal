## 1. Job
This page exists so that a first-time investor or goal planner who has decided to invest in mutual funds can see exactly which account they are opening (an investing account with Aikyam Wealth Private Limited, an AMFI-registered mutual fund distributor, ARN shown), that they do not need a trading account, what they pay and how Aikyam Wealth is paid, and what KYC involves, and start with their mobile number and an OTP, and leaves ready to complete KYC with the KYC partner on behalf of Aikyam Wealth.

Derivative of 09 Start Trading (golden, template D). Revision v3 (12-Oct-2026) inherits 09's **v3** treatments: hero C "Onboarding stage" (eyebrow, short h1, three proof points, a Mist stage with the step tracker and the compact form card), steps B (illustrated mini screens, icon tiles, two calm cards), the v3 FAQ split with an image slot, and the dark closing band with illustrative cards. It has no F&O block and no brokerage. The only layouts explored here are the hero (method rule: always) and the new "What will I pay?" block (`s-costs`).

## 3. Users and their anxieties
**U1 New and first-time investors** (primary)
1. "I only want a SIP. Do I have to open a trading or demat account?"
2. "Will I be charged things I do not understand? If it costs me nothing, how do they make money?"
3. "Is this a real, registered company, and if I type my number will I start getting sales calls?"

**U4 Goal planners** (retirement, a child's education, saving tax)
1. "Once the account is open, where do I actually start my SIP, and can I do it today?"
2. "Will they push me into the funds that pay them the most?"
3. "I already did KYC for another fund. Do I have to do it all again?"

Also reading this page: U9 diligence readers (Aikyam compliance treats it as an advertisement draft; distribution content is read against the AMFI code: ARN displayed, regular plans only, commission disclosed).

## 5. What this page must not say
- "Free", "zero" or "₹0" for opening the investing account as a word in copy. The account-opening stat tile shows `wealth-account-charge` in its numeral slot; a confirmed value (even ₹0) fills the slot with no other wording, beside the "Commission" tile (6.4 g; compliance to confirm). Never "zero commission", "commission-free", "no commission" or "Direct plans" (blocked: Aikyam Wealth earns commission on regular plans and cannot deal in Direct Plans).
- "No hidden charges", "lowest cost", "cheapest", "no surprises", "every cost", or any comparison with other platforms or distributors.
- Returns, projections or past performance in any form: no SIP growth example, no "wealth creation" or "grow your money" promise, no "beat FD", "safe", "guaranteed", "assured" or "risk-free". No tax figures (Section 80C amounts, "save up to ...") and no "tax-free".
- Real scheme names, fund houses or AMC logos; "best funds", "top-rated", "recommended", "handpicked", "curated" (blocked); counts of funds or fund houses available. Illustrative cards use example names only ("Example Flexi Cap Fund"), show no ₹ amounts, NAVs, returns or dates, and carry the caption "Illustrative screens. Example names, not real schemes."
- "Advisor", "adviser", "advice", "our experts pick", research language. "A real person to talk to when you need one" is the approved line. What help Aikyam Wealth may give in choosing funds stays a placeholder (`mf-guidance`).
- "SEBI-registered" for Aikyam Wealth: it is an AMFI-registered mutual fund distributor (ARN). Never show Aikyam Capital's SEBI number as if it covered this account.
- Legal company names in the h1, the lead or any h2 (v3). The entity sits in the eyebrow, the consent draft, the receiver fine print, card bodies that must name it (KYC card, costs tile and card), FAQ answers, the side doors, the s-final eyebrow and the footer.
- "One account for everything", "single account", or that this account can trade (Q7 open). The side door names Aikyam Capital.
- F&O words ("F&O", "futures", "options trading", "derivatives") anywhere: they are not this page's job and would call for the F&O risk box. The side door says "trade shares".
- "Fully online" as an absolute; durations ("in 5 minutes", "instant", "start your SIP today"); urgency or incentives for opening an account or starting a SIP (cashback, contests, "limited period").
- Security superlatives; no "secure" beyond the approved hand-off line.
- "Subject to ... exchange approval" for the investing account (it was written for the trading account; 6.4 e). No claim about where units are held ("in your demat", "in your name with the fund house") outside `mf-holding`.
- `{{url.login}}` (the trading platform) as the place to invest; registration numbers, the ARN or the KYC vendor typed literally (tokens only).
- Final consent or legal wording presented as approved: the consent line is Giraffe draft copy (`data-draft="consent-wealth"`) for compliance; no other contact promise ("we never call you"); escalation wording for distribution complaints stays a placeholder (`wealth-escalation`).
- App store links, QR codes or install claims (app coming soon); no phone mockup (site rule: Home and Get the App only).
- "IFA", "sub-broker".
- "Get OTP" on a link that only scrolls to the form: jump links read "Start with your mobile number" or "See what you pay".
- Links in the body to pages that are not live at the Phase 1 launch (2 Nov), except the commission disclosure (MUST question 5, with the go-live fallback in 6.4 j): no `/invest/mutual-funds`, `/invest/sip`, `/tools/sip-calculator`, `/plan/*` goal pages or `/login`.

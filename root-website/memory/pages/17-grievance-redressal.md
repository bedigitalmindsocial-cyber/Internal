## 1. Job
This page exists so that an existing client with a problem (or a diligence reader checking Aikyam's complaint routes) can see whom to write to, what to include, how long it takes and what it costs, how to escalate inside Aikyam, and how to take it to SEBI's SCORES or Smart ODR if they are not satisfied, and leaves ready to email the right Aikyam grievance desk (or, if they already have, to follow up, escalate, or file on SCORES or Smart ODR).

Golden page for template G: 16 Disclosures, 18 Escalation Matrix, 19 Policies, 20 Client Bank Accounts, 21 KYC Documents, 22 Risk Disclosure, 23 Notices, 24 Terms, 25 Privacy and 30 Commission Disclosure inherit its section layouts (6.9).

- **Primary action** (one, `data-primary`): hero button "Raise a complaint" to `#s-contents` (sitemap cta). `#s-contents` is the route band; the button lands on its head (the route order and the mutual fund fork), with Step 1 directly under it, where the grievance desk email, the demat address, the help desk phone and the checklist sit. Sitemap event `document_download` is the template G default and does not fit this action; proposed `complaint_start` (section 10, site proposal).

## 5. What this page must not say
- **Outcome or speed promises**: "we resolve every complaint", "resolved within 24 hours", "fast resolution", "guaranteed", "100% resolved", "zero pending complaints". Every timeline (acknowledgement, reply, SCORES and ODR stages) and every fee is a placeholder until compliance supplies it.
- **An order between SCORES and Smart ODR, or a precondition we cannot source** (judge): no "Step 4" after SCORES, no "escalate, then SEBI". When each portal may be used is compliance's to state (`scores-when`, `odr-when`); the hero and step copy say "or", not "then".
- **Regulatory or legal text drafted by Giraffe presented as official.** The plain-words route (hero lead, route order line, step paragraphs, the ladder's "when to write here" lines, the SCORES and ODR introduction, the follow-up line, the email template, the precedence line) is draft copy marked `data-draft` for compliance. The official procedure (SCORES review stages, ODR rules, time limits, fees) and the Grievance Redressal Policy text are client-supplied (`ph ph--block` in s-policy clauses 5 and 6 and the rest). No clause numbers or circular numbers from memory.
- **Numbers from memory**: no SEBI or exchange helpline numbers, no day counts, no fee amounts, no complaint counts or resolution rates (complaints data lives on 16 with an as-on date and source).
- **Guessed values inside placeholders** (judge 2): a placeholder names what is missing ("any charge, to confirm", "how often it is updated, to confirm"), never the value we expect ("nothing", "monthly"). The one value shown inside a placeholder is the help desk hours printed in the published Escalation Matrix (`contact-hours`), until it becomes a token.
- **Contacts typed literally**: every email, phone and registration value is a `{{token}}`; unknown ones are placeholders (level 2 phone, compliance email, hours, Aikyam Wealth complaints address). The main line `{{contact.phone}}` (listed against the CEO) never appears; no phone number for levels 3 and 4 on this page (18 carries the full matrix). The CEO is not named in the first screen.
- **Regulator branding or endorsement**: no SEBI, SCORES, Smart ODR, exchange or NSDL logos (borrowed regulator logos are banned); no "SEBI-approved", "SEBI protects you", or wording that suggests SEBI or the exchanges vouch for Aikyam. Portals are named in text with their address in mono.
- **Product promotion**: no "Open free account" or any product call to action in the body or the mobile bar (the header carries the site CTA; the mobile bar is set to "Raise a complaint"); no F&O, futures, options or derivatives words (they pull the F&O risk-box hint); mutual funds named only as "mutual fund distribution complaints" or "Is your complaint about mutual funds?" (lint will raise its MF-risk-line review hint; the page promotes no fund, so the line is not added, reviewer to confirm).
- **Requests for sensitive data**: neither the checklist nor the prefilled email asks for passwords, OTPs, full PAN, Aadhaar or bank numbers.
- **Advice or claim words**: "advice", "advisor", "free" (use "nothing, to confirm" inside the placeholder), "trusted", "best", "seamless".
- **Company names in the h1 or lead** (they sit in the eyebrow, card labels and fine print), **registration codes** outside the SCORES card in s-scores-odr and the footer.
- **Links to pages that are not live at Phase 1** in the body: /help/raise-ticket (Phase 3) is not linked; /help instead.
- **Hidden official text**: no accordion or "read more" around the official policy clauses (U9 must see them in full).

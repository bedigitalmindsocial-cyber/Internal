## 1. Job
This page exists so that a trader or first-time investor who has decided to open an account can see exactly which account they are opening (trading and demat, with Aikyam Capital Private Limited), what it costs, what KYC involves and what F&O needs, and start with their mobile number and an OTP, and leaves ready to complete KYC with Aikyam Capital's KYC partner.

Golden page for template D: 10 Start Investing, 11 Get the App, 74 Login and 75 Paid landing page inherit its section layouts (see 6.6).

## 2. Journey
- Arrives from: the "Open free account" button in the full header, hero and footer of every Capital and Both page (Home, Trade, Pricing, Equity, F&O, Currency and Commodity, IPO, About); the mobile bar of those pages; Home's hero lead bar (the number is carried over, see the OTP-sent state in 6.2); Google ("open trading and demat account online", "open demat account online", "open trading account"); aikyamcap.com's handoff to ROOT. Paid campaigns go to 75 /lp/open-account, not here.
- Awareness level: product-aware to ready to act. They chose to open an account; they now check that ROOT is a real broker, what it costs and what the process asks of them.
- Stage: Act.
- Previous page / next page(s): previous `/` (Home), `/trade`, `/pricing`, `/trade/futures-options`. Next: the OTP-sent state on this page, then the KYC partner's flow (host TO CONFIRM: ekyc.rootbyaikyam.com or the vendor URL), then `{{url.login}}` once the account is active. Side door: `/open-account/investing` (Aikyam Wealth) for visitors who only want to invest.
- Success:
  - Primary action: "Get OTP" (form submit in `#s-open`, marked `data-primary`, the only one on the page). Event `account_start` on a valid submit; downstream `otp_verified` and `kyc_submitted` fire on the KYC host.
  - Secondary action (at most one): "Investing only? Open an investing account with Aikyam Wealth" link to `/open-account/investing`. Event to define (proposed `switch_to_investing`).
  - Utility, not counted: Login in the minimal header.
- Search: query "open trading and demat account online". Title "Open a trading and demat account online | ROOT by Aikyam" (56 characters). Description "Open a trading and demat account with Aikyam Capital Private Limited, a SEBI-registered broker. Start with your mobile number and an OTP." (138 characters).

## 3. Users and their anxieties
**U2 Active traders** (primary; intraday, delivery, F&O, currency, commodity)
1. "What will I actually pay per order, brokerage plus all the other charges?"
2. "Can I get F&O switched on, and what proof will they ask for?"
3. "ROOT is new. Is this a real, registered broker I can check, or an app that disappears?"

**U1 New and first-time investors**
1. "Will this take long and ask for documents I don't have to hand?"
2. "If I type my number, will I start getting sales calls?"
3. "What if Aadhaar or PAN fails halfway? Do I lose everything, and is my data safe with them?"

Also reading this page: U9 diligence readers (Aikyam compliance treats it as an advertisement draft; exchanges may see it with filings).

## 5. What this page must not say
- "₹0", "free" or "zero" account opening, or "zero AMC", outside a placeholder: `{{pricing.account_opening}}` and `{{pricing.amc}}` are not confirmed. The primary button reads "Get OTP", never "Open free account".
- Any brokerage, DP or AMC figure, and never Motilal Oswal's rates as stand-ins. No "no hidden charges", "lowest brokerage", "cheapest". No "per order" in the brokerage list or copy: it presumes a flat-fee plan before the rate card exists (Q8).
- Durations or speed: "account in X minutes", "instant activation", "fast, reliable execution", "instant pledge", MTF or "pay later". Activation is always "subject to KYC verification and exchange approval" (plain-words version allowed in s-steps: "ready to use once your KYC is verified and the exchanges approve it").
- "Fully online" as an absolute (headings, lead, proof points) until the client tech head confirms that no step is offline. "Online" and "on your phone or computer" are fine.
- Security superlatives: "bank-grade security", "100% secure", "your data is never shared", "used only to"; no "secure" or "securely" beyond the approved hand-off line.
- F&O anywhere above the inset risk box in s-fo: not in the title, description, eyebrow, h1, lead, proof points, step cards or documents list (the lead says "add more segments when you're ready" without naming them). No F&O promotion at all ("trade F&O with high leverage", "multiply your capital"), and no F&O figures from memory (lot sizes, margins, expiry days, leverage).
- "One account for everything" or "single account" for trading and mutual funds (Q7 open). This page does not say mutual funds can be bought with this account; the side doors name Aikyam Wealth.
- The word "mutual fund" or "SIP" anywhere it is avoidable (it pulls the MF risk line in; the side door says "investing account").
- Advice or research language: tips, stock ideas, research reports, recommendations, advisor.
- Counts, ratings, awards, testimonials, "trusted by", comparisons with other brokers.
- Urgency or incentives for opening an account ("open today", "limited period", cashback, contests): prohibited for brokers.
- Contact promises beyond the consent draft: the consent line is Giraffe draft copy marked `data-draft="consent"` for compliance to approve (design direction v3); no other promise about calls ("we never call", "the box is never ticked for you").
- Legal company names in the h1, the lead or any h2 (v3: the entity sits in the eyebrow, the receiver line, the consent line, card bodies that must name it, side doors, fine print and the footer). Registration codes outside the hero proof points (the short footer carries the full strip).
- Prices, returns, P&L or real tickers in illustrative screens: example names only (Example Industries Ltd), masked personal data (98XXXXXX21, XXXXX1234X), no ₹ amounts.
- The KYC vendor's name typed literally (use `{{kyc.vendor}}`), the UAT login link (use `{{url.login}}`), registration numbers typed literally (tokens only).
- App store links, QR codes or install claims (app coming soon); no phone mockup on this page (site rule: Home and Get the App only). IPO claims beyond "apply for IPOs" in the hero ("apply in a few taps", UPI) are not this page's job.
- "Sub-broker", "IFA", "Direct plans".
- "Get OTP" on a link that only scrolls to the form: jump links read "Start with your mobile number" or "See what you pay".
- Links to pages that are not live at the Phase 1 launch (2 Nov): `/tools/margin-calculator` (Phase 2) and `/login` (Phase 3) in the page body. Login in the body uses `{{url.login}}`.

### 6.0 Diagnosis: why the pilot felt compliance-heavy, unbalanced, legal and text-heavy
- **s-hero, first screen:** two filled chips ("account opening charge to confirm" in the creds, "Consent wording from Aikyam compliance" in the card) and three registration codes appear before any benefit; five creds plus a 38-word lead that opens "A trading and demat account with Aikyam Capital Private Limited, the SEBI-registered stock broker behind ROOT by Aikyam". The h1 names a process, not an outcome. No product visual: the card is the only object, and the copy column ends at the creds while the card and side door run on (desktop-01: an empty lower-left area under the cost cred, top-aligned columns).
- **s-open:** the card stacks eight parts (step tag, h2, label, help, consent chip, receiver line, button, two-line hand-off); four of them read as legal text, and the receiver line repeats the SEBI number already shown in the creds.
- **s-risk:** a full-width warning band is the second thing on the page, directly under the form, with its own source chip. At 1440 it fills the second screen; at 390 it fills mobile-02. A first-time investor meets a loss statistic before learning how easy opening is.
- **s-steps:** text-only steps (dots and grey copy), then three Mist strips holding two large gold placeholder blocks (`kyc-fail`, `data-security`) and three inline chips (`kyc-duration`, `kyc-resume`, `kyc.vendor`). The process reads "to confirm" more than "easy", and desktop-02 has no visual anchor beyond the dots.
- **s-charges:** eight chips in one band (five identical "Rate card to confirm" cells in a table, three in the numeral card), the signature numeral reduced to a small chip, a 30-word statutory sentence, and a head line that names Aikyam Capital. A data table where tiles and a list would do.
- **s-fo:** "F&O needs one more step: proof of income." then a gold block of example documents and the line "They change often, so we do not print them here": legal tone, no visual.
- **s-faq:** the open item repeats the SEBI and DP codes from the hero and adds the proprietary line; items 2 and 3 repeat s-steps' placeholders word for word (`data-security`, `kyc-resume`, `kyc-fail`); the left column leads with the grievance email, framing the page around complaints before any question.
- **Whole page:** the legal company name appears about ten times in body copy, including the lead and the s-final h2 "Open your account with Aikyam Capital."; the hand-off line and the activation sentence each appear twice; 25 placeholders on the default page, 9 of them visible in the first two screens at 1440.

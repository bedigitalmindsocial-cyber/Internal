## 1. Job
This page exists so that someone checking whether ROOT is real (a new investor, an HNI or family, or a diligence reader at an exchange, partner firm or newsroom) can see which Aikyam companies stand behind ROOT, what each is registered for and how each is paid, who leads them, how to check every number and how to reach a person, and leaves ready to open a trading and demat account with Aikyam Capital (or, for mutual funds only, to start investing with Aikyam Wealth).

- **Primary action** (one, `data-primary`): the hero button "{{cta.primary}}" to /open-account/trading (sitemap cta). Final (judge 12-Oct-2026): a link, not a form (6.1; ask_user 1). The visitor types the mobile number on 09, whose form names Aikyam Capital as the receiver. Event: `account_start` (sitemap), fired by 09's form submit for visitors who arrive from this button; the click carries the site's shared CTA click event with location `about-hero` (name not fixed yet, section 10). If the judge or the user picks the inline bar instead (ask_user), the bar's submit fires `account_start` here, exactly as on Home.
- **Secondary action** (one): "Only mutual funds? Start investing" to /open-account/investing, in the Aikyam Wealth door and in s-final, `data-event="switch_to_investing"` (Home's event). `account_start` (entity wealth) fires only on page 10's submit.
- **In-page route (not counted)**: "See our registrations" anchor to #s-verify, beside the hero button.
- **Search**: main query "Aikyam Capital Group". Secondary: "who owns ROOT by Aikyam", "is ROOT by Aikyam SEBI registered", "ROOT by Aikyam genuine". The brand query "ROOT by Aikyam" belongs to Home (01); "Aikyam Capital" and "Aikyam Capital Private Limited" belong to 12.
  - Title (66 characters): "About ROOT by Aikyam: the Aikyam Capital Group companies behind it"
  - Description (146 characters): "ROOT by Aikyam is the retail brand of Aikyam Capital Group. See which company does what, check our registrations yourself and reach a real person."

## 5. What this page must not say
- **Scale, age and proof**: no investor, client, download, AUM or transaction counts; no founding year, "since", "years of experience" or "decades"; no awards, ratings, stars, testimonials, press logos or "as seen in"; no "trusted by", "leading", "largest" or any comparison with another broker. A milestone timeline is out (no approved dates; never compute a year from a CIN).
- **People**: names and titles exactly as written in the corporate profile (facts.md section 10); no bios, quotes, education or past employers on this page (bios only from approved text, on 14 Leadership); no photos except `figure.img-slot`; no founder's note.
- **ROOT as a company**: ROOT is a brand. Never "ROOT is SEBI-registered" or "ROOT Private Limited"; registrations always belong to a named company.
- **Group lines**: investment banking and asset management are named as group lines only, with no company names (open question). Never imply that the group's asset management line runs anything offered on ROOT. PMS on ROOT is "from third-party SEBI-registered portfolio managers, empanelled for distribution" and Coming soon; never "our PMS". Aikyam Capital Management LLP and its PM registration are not mentioned.
- **AMFI is not a regulator**: it is an industry association (compliance.md section 1). Say "the official website that issued it", never "each regulator's website" for a list that includes AMFI. The ARN is a registration, not a licence.
- **F&O**: the page is not flagged `fo_risk`. No "F&O", "futures", "options" or "derivatives" anywhere in main, title or description. Aikyam Capital's services read "Buy shares and ETFs, apply for IPOs, and add more segments when you're ready." Exchange names (NSE, BSE, MCX, MSEI) are facts, not promotion.
- **Unconfirmed theme claims** (claims.json): none of the theme pillars ("Fast, reliable execution", "Open-architecture research", "Institutional-grade research", "Bank-grade security"); the theme About heading "Built to earn your trust, not just your trades." is not reused; no "secure" beyond the approved KYC hand-off line; no "safe", "100% safe" or "your money is safe".
- **Advice and research**: no advisor, adviser, advice, research, tips, stock ideas or recommendations; no "expert guidance".
- **Mutual fund distribution**: never "Direct plans", "zero commission" or "commission-free"; never omit the ARN where mutual funds are offered (Wealth door fine print, Wealth register); regular plans only.
- **Insurance**: not mentioned (licensing company and class unconfirmed).
- **Accounts**: no "one account", "single account" or "everything" (Q7 open); no "free" outside `{{cta.primary}}`; no ₹0 as plain text.
- **Contacts**: customer-facing phone is `{{contact.helpdesk_phone}}` only. Never the main line (listed against the CEO), the compliance officer's line or the CEO's email; no escalation levels 3 and 4 on this page (they live on 18). No corporate or regional office addresses (Q10 open).
- **Codes**: every registration value as a `{{token}}`, never typed. No codes in h2s, door headers or the contact strip. In main: the SEBI code in the hero proof point and the Capital register; every other code once, in its register; the ARN also in the Wealth door fine print.
- **Company names in headlines (v3)**: never in the h1, the lead or any h2. They live in the eyebrows, door h3s, register h3s, fine print and the footer.
- **Caution notice**: no promises the client has not approved ("we will never call you", "we never ask for your OTP"). The plain-words version is a draft (`data-draft="caution-plain"`).
- **Wording traps**: no em dashes, no "click here", no "sub-broker" or "IFA", no urgency.

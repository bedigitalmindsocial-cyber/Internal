## 1. Job
This page exists so that an HNI, a family, a professional or a business owner who is weighing portfolio management services can see what PMS is, whether it fits them (the SEBI minimum of {{pms.minimum}}, the risk, fees, exit loads and lock-ins), who would manage their money (a third-party, SEBI-registered portfolio manager) and what Aikyam Wealth does (distributes strategies empanelled for distribution, and does not manage or hold the money), and leaves having asked the Aikyam Wealth team to call them ("Talk to the wealth team"), which also puts them on the list to hear when PMS goes live on ROOT.

- **What the page is**: PMS is not live on ROOT. This is a Phase 3 (13 Jan 2027) template H page with the Coming soon badge. It is the golden page for template H: 51 to 56 and 58 to 60 inherit its layouts (6.8).
- **Primary action** (one, `data-primary`): the hero notify bar (pattern "Hero inline bar form", 01 v3, relabelled): +91 field and "Talk to the wealth team" (sitemap `primary_cta`) in one row, the unticked required consent box (draft `consent-wealth-request`, 6.4), receiver and next-step lines as `p.fine`. Data receiver: Aikyam Wealth Private Limited. The visitor stays on the page (success state); no OTP (section 10).

## 5. What this page must not say
- **In-house PMS**: never "our PMS", "ROOT PMS", "Aikyam PMS", "our strategies", "our fund managers", "managed by Aikyam". Aikyam Capital Management LLP's own co-investment PMS registration (compliance.md section 3) is a different entity and never appears. Always "empanelled for distribution" and "third-party, SEBI-registered portfolio managers".
- **Named providers or products before clearance** (coming_soon): no real portfolio manager, strategy, AMC or custodian names; fictional names only inside the captioned illustrative card ("Example Portfolio Managers Ltd", "Example Equity Strategy").
- **Performance and outcomes**: no returns, CAGR, alpha, "beat the market", "outperform", benchmarks, drawdowns, "top performing", "consistent", "wealth creation", "compounding" promises, projections or example portfolio values. No "guaranteed", "assured", "capital protection", "safe".
- **Superlatives and advice**: no "best PMS", "top PMS", "handpicked", "curated", "carefully selected", "recommended strategies", "expert picks", "advisor", "adviser", "advisory PMS", "investment advice", "model portfolio", "stock ideas". Do not describe how Aikyam Wealth selects managers (due-diligence claims) until `pms-empanelment` is confirmed.
- **Numbers not confirmed**: no fee percentages, hurdle rates, exit load or lock-in periods, distributor commission, launch date, number of managers or strategies. The SEBI minimum only as {{pms.minimum}}; never the ₹25 lakh consultation figure (not law), and no accredited-investor exception (section 10).
- **Exclusivity and status**: no "exclusive", "elite", "private wealth", "privileged", "by invitation", "for the few", no luxury imagery; no urgency ("register early", "limited slots", "be first").
- **Sales promises about the call**: no call-back times ("within 24 hours"), no "dedicated relationship manager", no "free consultation" (the "free" review term) and no "personalised advice".
- **Platform claims**: no statement that PMS holdings will show in the ROOT app or web platform, no "track everything in one place", no "one account".
- **Tax claims**: no "tax-efficient", no statement about how PMS gains are taxed.
- **Company names and codes (v3)**: no company name in the h1, lead or any h2; the AMFI ARN only as {{reg.wealth.arn}} in fine print and the disclosure band; no SEBI number of Aikyam Capital presented as covering PMS.
- **The call as advice or a needs assessment** (judge): no "understand what you are looking for", "find the right strategy for you", "help you choose a strategy", "assess your needs". The call answers questions about PMS and helps with the forms.
- **Links that promise lists that do not exist** (judge): no "See empanelled strategies", "Meet the managers" or "Browse strategies" until 58 and 59 list real ones.
- **Wording traps**: no em dashes, spaced en dashes, "click here", "IFA", "sub-broker", "Direct plans".

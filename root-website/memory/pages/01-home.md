## 1. Job
This page exists so that a first-time visitor (new investor, active trader, IPO applicant, goal planner or HNI) can see what ROOT offers, which Aikyam company provides each part, what it costs and what happens when they start, and leaves ready to open a trading and demat account with Aikyam Capital (or, for mutual funds only, to start investing with Aikyam Wealth).

- **Primary action** (one, `data-primary`): the hero inline mobile bar (v3; the 09 form card in v1): +91 field and "{{cta.primary}}" in one row, with the unticked consent box, the receiver line and the hand-off as fine print under it, posting to /open-account/trading, which opens in its OTP-sent state with the number and consent carried over. Data receiver: Aikyam Capital Private Limited. Event: `account_start` (entity: capital).
- **Secondary action** (one): "Only mutual funds? Start investing with Aikyam Wealth" link to /open-account/investing (site memory, red-team round 1). Data receiver on that page: Aikyam Wealth Private Limited. Event on Home: `switch_to_investing` (a link click, the name 09 proposes); `account_start` (entity: wealth) fires only on page 10's form submit, so starts are not counted twice (judge).
- **Search**: main query "ROOT by Aikyam" (navigational brand query; "open demat account online" belongs to 09 Start Trading, so Home does not compete for it).
  - Title (64 characters): "ROOT by Aikyam: open a trading account or invest in mutual funds"
  - Description (150 characters): "Open a trading and demat account with Aikyam Capital, or invest in mutual funds through Aikyam Wealth. See the charges and the steps before you start."
  - Neither title nor description names F&O (the hero must not promote F&O ahead of the risk box, see section 6).

## 2. Journey
- Arrives from: brand search ("ROOT by Aikyam", "root aikyam", "Aikyam Capital demat"); the parent site aikyamcap.com, which hands retail traffic to ROOT (facts.md section 1); press and social mentions; partner referrals (Authorised Persons, distributors). Paid campaigns land on /lp/open-account (75), not here. Existing clients (U7) arrive to log in through the header.
- Awareness level: mostly solution-aware (they want to invest or trade and are checking this provider); brand searchers and aikyamcap.com hand-offs are product-aware.
- Stage: Discover, then Evaluate.
- Previous page / next page(s): previous is a search result page, aikyamcap.com or a referral link. Next: /open-account/trading (primary, 09, arriving in its OTP-sent state), /open-account/investing (secondary, 10), then supporting paths /pricing (06), /about (07), /trade (03), /invest (02), /plan (04), /help (08), /app (11), /login (74). Every section has at least one link to a Phase 1 page (live 2 Nov), so no section is a dead end at launch (judge).

## 3. Users and their anxieties
- **U1 new investors**: "Is this a real, registered company or a fake app?" · "Will I be charged things I do not understand?" · "I only want a SIP. Do I need a trading account?"
- **U2 active traders**: "What is the brokerage per order, and what else gets added?" · "Can I trade F&O as soon as I open?" · "Who holds my money and my shares?"
- **U3 IPO applicants**: "Can I apply for IPOs here, and with which company?" · "Does applying cost anything?" · "Where do I check allotment?" (Phase 2 pages)
- **U4 goal planners**: "Where do I start for retirement or my child's education?" · "Are these returns promised?" · "Which fund should I pick?" (the site may not tell them: no advice)
- **U5 HNIs and families**: "Is there a real person for larger money?" · "Is PMS run in-house or by someone else?" · "Who is behind this group?"
- Also present though not in the audience list: U9 diligence readers ("Which company holds which licence, and are the disclosures in place?") and U7 clients looking for Login.

## 5. What this page must not say
- **"One account" for everything.** The theme h1 "Trade & invest in everything, from one account." and the band line "One account covers all six" assume one onboarding flow, which is open (Q7) and contradicted by two companies and two account doors. No "one account" or "single account" anywhere until Q7 is answered (claims.json review). "Everything" is also an overclaim (no US stocks; PMS, bonds, NPS and insurance are not live).
- **F&O ahead of the risk box.** The hero (eyebrow, h1, lead, form copy, proof points, product stage) and the title and description do not name F&O, futures, options or derivatives; nor do the s-products h2 and the Trade door h3, which sit above the inset box. F&O first appears in the Trade door, directly under the inset risk box (Gate 1). No lot sizes, margins, expiries or eligibility thresholds from memory; link to nseindia.com.
- **Unconfirmed theme claims** (claims.json "unconfirmed"; voice-and-claims.md): "Fast, reliable execution", "Bank-grade security", "Open-architecture" or "Institutional-grade research" (all three theme pillars), "instant pledge and MTF" (theme Stocks card), "₹0 account opening" as plain text (only as {{pricing.account_opening}}), "zero brokerage", "free AMC".
- **Scale, proof and urgency**: no investor, client, download or AUM counts, years in business, awards, ratings, stars, testimonials or media logos; no "trusted by"; no "act now" or "limited period"; no comparison with any broker.
- **Advice and research**: no advisor, adviser or advice (except "not investment advice"), no research, stock ideas, tips, target prices, model portfolios, "AI-led"; no named schemes as picks; no "curated", "handpicked", "best" or "top-rated" funds. Fund names only as illustrative examples in the phone mockup.
- **Mutual fund distribution**: never "Direct plans", "zero commission" or "commission-free"; never omit the ARN; regular plans only.
- **PMS**: never in-house; only "third-party SEBI-registered portfolio managers, empanelled for distribution by Aikyam Wealth", marked Coming soon. If the SEBI minimum is shown it is {{pms.minimum}}; never the ₹25 lakh consultation figure.
- **Insurance**: Coming soon only, with no company named (licensing entity TBC) and no product claims.
- **App**: no store links, QR code, install counts, ratings or "download now"; no feature list (the app feature spec is not in hand); no speed claims such as "in a few taps".
- **Illustrative product visuals (v3; replaces the v1 phone rule)**: the hero product stage may show the theme phone (portfolio value, holdings split, one example SIP row) and two floating cards: an IPO application card (Example Industries Ltd, an illustrative price band, "Pay with UPI", an Apply chip) and a SIP set-up card (Example Flexi Cap Fund, amount and date, a Start SIP chip). s-steps may show one KYC step card (sample PAN ABCDE1234F, no prices). Never a goal-progress card or Goals tab, alerts, margin, MTF or pledge, real tickers or index levels, returns or projections. Fictional names only; chips in illustrations are spans, not buttons; "Illustrative data. Not real prices or returns." directly under any visual with prices or values.
- **Market data**: no real index or stock names with invented values (theme ticker and markets module). Illustrative numbers only inside the hero product stage, with example names and the illustrative label directly under it.
- **Projections**: no SIP or return projection computed by Giraffe on this page (compliance.md section 1: "No figure computed by Giraffe").
- **Wording traps**: no "sub-broker" or "IFA" (partner links live in the footer only), no "secure" beyond the approved KYC hand-off line, no "fully online" as an absolute (the steps are indicative), no em dashes, no "click here".
- **Company names in headlines (v3)**: never in the h1, the lead or any h2. They live in the eyebrow, door labels, stat-tile labels, the receiver line, fine print, FAQ answers and the footer.
- **Registration codes per section (v3)**: not in door headers or FAQ answers. In main: the hero proof point and receiver line (SEBI), the Invest door fine print (ARN), the three Check it yourself cards and the disclosure band.
- **Consent wording**: a Giraffe draft marked `data-draft="consent-mobile"` until compliance supplies the text; no contact promises ("we never call you", "the box is never ticked for you").
- **Absolute cost claims**: "Know what you pay before you start." is a promise the Pricing page keeps; never "no hidden charges", "all charges" or "every charge" (fix 12-Oct-2026: the s-pricing lead reads "Our charges are listed on one public page, so you can check what you pay before you start.").

### 6.1 Diagnosis of the v1 build (from the 1440 and 390 captures)
1. **The first screen reads as a KYC form, not a product (s-hero).** The right column is a ten-part form card (step label, heading, field, help, consent chip, receiver line, button, two-sentence hand-off, cost line with a chip, link), then the side door and the MF risk line. Three gold chips sit in the first screen (`consent-mobile`, `pricing.account_opening`, the ARN in the creds). The only product visual on the page, the phone, is in s-final, about 6,600 px down.
2. **Unbalanced at 1440 (s-hero).** Columns are top-aligned; the left column ends at y 610 while the card and side door run to y 840, leaving an empty quadrant under the creds. At 390 the primary button sits at y 1,001.
3. **Legal names lead the copy (s-hero, s-products, s-group).** The lead names both companies; the s-products h2 is two company names ("Trade with Aikyam Capital. Invest with Aikyam Wealth.") followed by a meta line ("Each product names the company that provides it."); door headers are legal names with registration codes; s-group opens with a 40-word legal paragraph. All four hero creds are registration statements.
4. **Compliance comes second (s-risk).** A full-width, alarm-style box with an alert icon and a 64-character compliance chip is the second thing every visitor sees (bottom of screen 1 at 1440, all of screen 2 at 390), before they know what ROOT offers.
5. **Registration codes everywhere.** In main the SEBI code appears 5 times (hero cred, receiver line, Trade door, verify ledger, FAQ), the ARN 6 times (each a chip) and the DP code twice, on top of the footer strip.
6. **The least finished screen is a table of chips (s-pricing).** Eight rows in which every rate is a "to confirm" chip, a caption "(rate card to be confirmed)", a ninth chip in the Wealth card and an empty right half under it. Main carries 23 placeholders overall.
7. **Text without visual anchors (s-products, s-steps, s-faq).** Only 2 of about 9 screen heights at 1440 have a visual (group map, phone). The doors are two text lists with a half-empty Invest door; steps and FAQ are text only; the FAQ's left column is empty below its links; the coming-soon row is four bordered boxes with badges.
8. **Sentences sound like filings.** "Account and brokerage charges (rate card to be confirmed)", "Exchange, clearing and company codes for both companies are in the footer.", "Who is behind ROOT." Headings state the page's structure instead of the visitor's question or a benefit.

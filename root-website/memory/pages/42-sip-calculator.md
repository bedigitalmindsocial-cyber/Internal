## 1. Job
This page exists so that a first-time investor or goal planner can estimate what a monthly SIP could add up to under assumptions they set and can see, check the maths behind the number, understand that it is an estimate and not a forecast, and leaves ready to start a SIP through Aikyam Wealth Private Limited by opening an investing account ("Start a SIP" to /open-account/investing).

- **What the page is**: the tool is the hero (Motilal ADOPT: "SIP calculator: the tool is the hero, the SEO article sits below"). Golden for template C: 43 Lumpsum, 44 Retirement, 45 Brokerage, 46 Margin, 47 SPAN, 48 Risk Profiler, 49 Retirement Planning and 50 Child Education Planning inherit its layouts (6.6). Phase 2, live 8 Dec 2026 with the hub (41) and the Wealth pages it links (10 is live from 2 Nov; 28, 30, 48 to 50 go live with it).
- **Primary action** (one, `data-primary`): the link-button "Start a SIP" at the foot of the result card (`section#s-result`, nested in the hero) to `/open-account/investing` (sitemap `cta`). No form on this page: page 10 collects the mobile number, consent and OTP for Aikyam Wealth (6.1 row 4). The click carries `calc_start` (params `tool=sip`, amount, years, rate) until the orchestrator settles 07's shared click-event question; `account_start` (the sitemap event) fires on page 10's submit, as for 07's link button (section 10).
- **Secondary action** (one): "Check your risk profile" to `/tools/risk-profiler` (s-next step 2 and the s-final outline button), proposed event `tool_switch` (`to=risk-profiler`). It answers the next worry after "how much" ("which kind of fund?") without advice. Fallback (judge): if 48 is not live on 8 Dec (licence_check), both links become "Try the lumpsum calculator" (/tools/lumpsum-calculator) with no layout change; 48's own sitemap action opens an Aikyam Capital account, which section 10 flags.
- **Other routes to the same action** (never `data-primary`): the s-next band button, the s-final light button, the mobile bar (front matter default, "Start a SIP" to /open-account/investing) and the header button (shell; Wealth entity).

## 3. Users and their anxieties
**U1 New and first-time investors** (primary)
1. "I don't know what return to put in. Is 12% realistic, or is that what they want me to believe?"
2. "Is this big number real, or is the page just trying to sell me something?"
3. "Can I lose money in a SIP? What if markets fall?"

**U4 Goal planners**
1. "How much do I need to put in every month to reach my goal?"
2. "Does this allow for inflation and tax? Real numbers are always lower."
3. "Can I check the maths myself, or is it a black box?"

Also reading: U9 diligence readers (compliance officers and AMFI-minded reviewers check the assumptions, the caption, the ARN and that no rate reads as a forecast or a suggestion); U7 existing ROOT investors planning a second SIP ("Where do I set it up?").

## 5. What this page must not say
- **Promises about the result**: "will grow to", "you will get", "your SIP will become", "double your money", "beat inflation", "beat FD", "wealth creation" as an outcome, "safe", "secure returns", "guaranteed" (blocked except in the negated theme line "Mutual fund returns are not guaranteed."; never in a heading or a FAQ question, even asked: judge), "assured", "risk-free", "capital protection". The estimate is always "estimated" or "could"; the h1 says "could add up to", never "will".
- **A default rate dressed as evidence or advice**: "expected return" (the label is "Assumed return"), "typical", "average", "historical", "long-term average", "equity funds have returned 12%", "suggested rate", "recommended rate", "realistic rate", or rate presets mapped to fund categories or risk levels ("Conservative 8%, Aggressive 15%"). No past returns of any real scheme, category or index (no NIFTY, Sensex or real fund data).
- **Funds as advice**: no scheme names in the tool or the steps, no "best SIP plans", "top SIP funds", "SIP plans to invest in", "recommended funds", ranks or ratings (claims.json blocked list). Categories only (Equity, Debt, Hybrid). No scheme name anywhere on the page, not even an example name, and no fund category next to a rate or a projection (judge: the s-final SIP card reads "Fund: You choose"; 10 keeps its "Example Flexi Cap Fund" card).
- **Giraffe-computed figures beyond the formula**: no "cost of delay" device ("start 5 years later and you lose ₹X": an urgency device and a Giraffe figure), no "what ₹X became" history, no comparison with FDs, gold or property, no inflation-adjusted value (no inflation rate is approved), no figure other than the exact arithmetic of the stated formula on the visitor's own inputs.
- **Tax**: no rates, holding periods, exemption limits, Section 80C, ELSS or "tax-free" (64 Tax Saving owns that). The FAQ says tax is not taken off, with `mf-tax`.
- **Unconfirmed ROOT facts**: step-up or top-up SIP, pause, date choice, "SIP from ₹100/₹500" or any ROOT minimum, auto-debit method, where investors log in, "track it in the ROOT app" (app coming soon), that the amount carries over into onboarding (until confirmed), the account charge and transaction charge (placeholders), that no demat account is needed (only "No trading account needed" is confirmed, 10's wording; where units are held is `mf-holding`).
- **Plans and commission**: "Direct plan" in any form (blocked), "zero commission", "commission-free", "free" (review term; the calculator copy says "No charge, no sign-up" and "with no sign-up").
- **Advice words**: "advisor", "adviser", "advice", "expert", "research", "our recommendation"; ROOT "doesn't suggest a rate or a fund".
- **Company names (v3)**: no legal company name in the h1, the hero lead or any h2. The entity sits in the result card's fine print, s-next step 1 and card, FAQ 7, the s-final eyebrow, the disclosure band and the footer. "SEBI-registered" never describes Aikyam Wealth (AMFI-registered distributor, ARN). Codes only as `{{tokens}}`.
- **Motion and devices**: no counting-up result, no animated chart draw-in on load, no pulsing button, no urgency ("start today", "don't wait"), no status by colour alone (chart series labelled in text at their ends).
- **Other**: F&O words, "one account", "single account", "IFA", "sub-broker", em dashes, spaced en dashes (the formula uses the minus sign "−", U+2212), "click here", "10 years of ..." phrasing (lint reads "N years of" as a scale claim; write "for 10 years", "after 10 years").

## 1. Job
This page exists so that a first-time investor (U1) can understand, in plain words, what they can invest in, which ROOT account they need and which Aikyam company provides it, what it costs, what opening an account involves and how a first investment works, and leaves ready to open a trading and demat account with Aikyam Capital Private Limited ("Open free account" to /open-account/trading), or an investing account with Aikyam Wealth Private Limited if they only want mutual funds.

- **What the page is**: the beginner's front door to ROOT's Learn section and the golden page for template E (Article). 64 Tax Saving, 66 Guide: Mutual Funds, 67 Guide: PMS, 68 Guide: Insurance, 69 Guide: Trading Basics, 70 Glossary and 72 Press and Media inherit its layouts (6.6). Phase 3 in sitemap.json (live 13 Jan 2027), but the Learn hub (05, Phase 1) has "Read the getting started guide" as its primary action and Home's FAQ links here from 2 Nov (section 10, ask_user 1).
- **Primary action** (one, `data-primary`): the hero button `{{cta.primary}}` ("Open free account") to `/open-account/trading` (sitemap `cta`, form entity Aikyam Capital). No form on this page: 09 collects the mobile number, consent and OTP. Proposed click event `guide_start` (params `guide=getting-started`, `position=hero`); `account_start` stays on 09's submit, as on Home and 42 (section 10).
- **Secondary action** (one): "Only mutual funds? Start investing" to `/open-account/investing` (hero side link, Investing door button, s-final outline button), event `switch_to_investing` (Home's name). It serves the U1 visitor who only wants a SIP, without a second primary.
- **Other routes to the same action** (never `data-primary`): the Trading door button in s-account, the s-open band button, the s-final light button, the mobile bar (front matter default) and the header button (shell). Each carries `data-event="guide_start"` with its own `position`.
- **Search**: main query "how to start investing"; supporting "how to start investing in india for beginners", "investing for beginners", "demat account vs trading account", "documents required for demat account", "stocks vs mutual funds for beginners", "how much money do i need to start investing".

## 3. Users and their anxieties
**U1 New and first-time investors** (the only audience in sitemap.json)
1. "I don't know where to start, and every site throws jargon at me." (demat, KYC, NAV, SIP, ETF)
2. "Can I lose my money? I can't afford to get this wrong."
3. "Two companies, two accounts: is this complicated, and are they legit?"

Also: "What will it cost me, and are there charges I won't see coming?"; "I don't know what to buy, and I don't want to be pushed into something."

Also reading: U9 diligence readers (compliance and exchange reviewers check that a broker's education page gives no advice, names the right company for each product and carries the MF and distributor lines); U4 goal planners passing through to the calculators and the MF guide; U7 existing clients looking for "how do I make my first investment".

## 5. What this page must not say
- **Advice or recommendations**: no "best", "top", "recommended", "should buy", "start with an index fund", "SIPs are the best way", allocation splits, "beginner-friendly stocks or funds", "stock ideas", "tips" (blocked even in "beginner tips"), "advisor", "advice", "expert", "research". ROOT "doesn't recommend shares or funds". The "questions to ask yourself" in s-first are questions with no numbers and no answers chosen for the reader (draft `guide-questions`, section 10).
- **Personal-finance rules with numbers**: no "keep six months of expenses aside", "invest 20% of your salary", "start with ₹500", "the 50/30/20 rule": invented numbers and advice.
- **Minimums and custody (judge)**: the s-basics "Start with" rows and FAQ 1 name units only (one share, one ETF unit, one IPO lot, the scheme's minimum), never a rupee amount. The demat line says only where shares are held ("in your name, at NSDL (a depository)", draft `demat-nsdl`); never "safe", "protected" or "secure" about holdings.
- **Promises and returns**: no "grow your wealth", "beat inflation", "beat FDs", "power of compounding" figures, "safe", "secure", "guaranteed", "assured", "risk-free", "capital protection", "long-term investing always pays", "SIPs reduce risk" or "rupee cost averaging protects you". No past returns of any share, fund, index or category.
- **F&O**: no futures, options, F&O, derivatives, intraday leverage, margin, MTF or "add more segments" anywhere on the page. The page has no fo_risk flag; 69 Guide: Trading Basics owns derivatives with the risk box. Its related card names it "Trading basics" and describes buying and selling shares only.
- **Unconfirmed ROOT facts**: "₹0", "zero" or "free" account opening or AMC (tokens only; "free" is a review term), "fully online", "instant", "in 10 minutes", the KYC vendor's name typed literally, UPI for IPOs, how money is added (placeholder `fund-account`), app availability, store links or "download the app", NRI, joint, minor or HUF accounts, bonds, NPS, PMS or insurance availability, "one account", "single account", "one sign-up for both".
- **Plans and commission**: "Direct plan" in any form, "zero commission", "commission-free". "SEBI-registered" never describes Aikyam Wealth (AMFI-registered distributor).
- **Tax**: no rates, holding periods, 80C, ELSS or "tax-free" (64 Tax Saving owns tax).
- **Company names (v3)**: no legal company name in the h1, the hero lead or any h2; they sit in the door labels, fine print, FAQ answers, the s-final eyebrow, the disclosure band and the footer. Registration codes only as `{{tokens}}`, and only in the door feet (the hero has no proof points on this page).
- **Data and imagery**: no real tickers, scheme names, fund houses, prices, NAVs or dates; illustrative cards use "Example Industries Ltd" (order card) and a scheme-free SIP card ("Fund: You choose", no category; judge) with a caption. No stock photos of traders at screens.
- **Counts and scale**: no investor, client or download counts; numbers before nouns such as "accounts", "funds" or "schemes" are written as words ("two accounts") so lint does not read them as scale claims; never "N years of".
- **Other**: em dashes, spaced en dashes, "click here", "seamless", "unlock", "empower", "IFA", "sub-broker", urgency ("start today", "don't wait").

### 6.5 Template E conventions (proposed site rules; every derivative follows them)
- **Hero**: eyebrow "Guide · {topic}" (70 "Glossary", 72 "Press and media"); h1 the outcome with the query's words; a lead that says who the guide is for (never a list of the parts: the route card shows them); one primary action plus at most one side link, each with its company or risk line in `p.fine`; right a stage panel with the route card (chapters as jump links, read time in its head, "Last updated" in its foot fine print).
- **Chapters**: one band per chapter, alternating Mist and white; `span.label` "Part N of M"; h2 `d2 node` as the visitor's question; 66ch lead; one visual block; at most one callout; `a.link.chapter-next` at the foot (not on the last chapter).
- **Prose**: running text sits in `.prose` (max 66ch, Mukta body size, h3 subheads, lists). Tables only when readers compare three or more attributes: `table.data.data--stack` with `td-label`s and a visible caption.
- **Callouts**: `aside.callout` and `.callout--term` for notes and jargon only, one or two sentences; costs, eligibility and other MUST answers sit in cards or row lists (judge). `.callout--term` links `/learn/glossary#term-{slug}` once 70 provides anchors; never the risk-box look (reserved for the F&O box).
- **Costs**: one card with a labelled row list per company, in the doors' order, beside a receipt when statutory charges apply (judge).
- **Read time**: words in main ÷ 200, rounded up, computed by the builder; "Last updated" is a placeholder until compliance dates each guide.
- **Not a recommendation**: the `guide-not-advice` line in every guide's disclosure band; no product is ever suggested; illustrative cards use example names with a caption, and fund cards name no scheme or category (judge).
- **Company lines**: the account action names its company in `p.fine` next to it; registration codes appear once per guide, where the visitor acts (door feet here).
- **No F&O words** on a guide without the fo_risk flag; 69 puts `#s-risk` as the second section (lint), before its route card's first chapter.

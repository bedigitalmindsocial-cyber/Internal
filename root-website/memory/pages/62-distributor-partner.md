## 1. Job
This page exists so that a mutual fund distributor (an individual or a firm holding an ARN from AMFI, or someone preparing for NISM Series V-A) can see what partnering with ROOT through Aikyam Wealth involves: who qualifies, how payouts are worked out and what joining costs, the steps from application to the first client, what partners agree to and must never do, and which company signs the agreement under which registration. They leave having applied (mobile number) so the Aikyam Wealth partner team can call them.

- **What the page is**: a Phase 3 (13 Jan 2027) partner recruitment page, marketing plus lead form only (site memory: no partner portal build). It is the golden page for template P: 61 Partner with ROOT and 63 Authorised Person Programme inherit its layouts (6.6).
- **Primary action** (one, `data-primary`): the submit of the apply form in `section.form-card#s-apply`, nested in s-hero (sitemap cta `#s-apply`), label "Apply as a distributor partner" (sitemap `primary_cta`). `form` with `method="post"`, `data-event="partner_lead"`, `data-programme="distributor"`, `data-form-entity="Aikyam Wealth"`. Data receiver: Aikyam Wealth Private Limited. No OTP (section 10). The visitor stays on the page (success state).
- **Secondary action** (one): "See the Authorised Person Programme" to /partner/authorised-person (Aikyam Capital), event `switch_to_ap` (new name, site proposal). It sits under the hero stage (`.hero-alt`), in FAQ 6 and in s-final.
- **Other routes to the primary action** (plain links to `#s-apply`, never `data-primary`): the s-eligibility card link, the s-steps band button, the s-final light button, the FAQ lead link and the mobile bar (front matter `mbar` "Apply to partner", section 10). The header button is the shell's {{cta.primary}} today; the judge proposes that template P headers follow the page's cta ("Apply to partner" to `#s-apply`), a shell change for the orchestrator (ask_user, section 10).
- **Search**: main query "mutual fund distributor partner programme". Supporting queries answered on the page: "how to become a mutual fund distributor", "NISM Series V-A ARN EUIN", "mutual fund distributor commission", "mutual fund distributor platform". Searches that use the barred word for this category are not targeted (site naming rule).

## 3. Users and their anxieties
- **U6 practising mutual fund distributors** (individual ARN holders, often 35 to 55, serving families and small business owners in cities such as Ludhiana, Pune or Indore):
  1. "If my clients invest through ROOT, are they still my clients, or will Aikyam call them directly?"
  2. "What share of the commission do I get, when is it paid, and can I see each payout?"
  3. "Is there a joining fee, a deposit or a target, and can I leave if it does not work out?"
  - Also: "What will my clients see and use?", "Can I bring my existing clients?", "What tools and support do I get?"
- **U6 new entrants** (insurance agents, former bank relationship managers, young professionals preparing for NISM Series V-A or newly holding an ARN): "I do not have my ARN yet. Can I still start with you?", "What exactly do I need, and in what order?"
- **U6 distribution firms with staff** (firm ARN): "Can my whole team work under one agreement, and what does each person need?"
- **Also present**: Authorised Persons who want to add mutual funds (routed to the right programme by FAQ 6 and the side door); U9 diligence readers (fund houses, AMFI, compliance teams) checking the ARN display, the code of conduct, the absence of earnings claims and of barred titles.

## 5. What this page must not say
- **Barred titles**: no "IFA", "independent financial adviser", "wealth adviser", "adviser", "advisor", "advice", "advisory", and no "sub-broker" or "sub-distributor", including AMFI's form field name for a distributor under another ARN. The conduct rule is phrased "Use a title other than mutual fund distributor" so the barred words never appear (no `lint_allow` needed).
- **Earnings claims**: no "earn up to", "passive income", "unlimited earnings", "income for life", "lifetime trail", "high", "attractive", "best" or "industry-leading" payouts, no earnings examples, AUM-to-income examples or calculators, no "upfront" commission talk.
- **Numbers not confirmed**: no payout percentages or slabs, payout dates or cycle, joining fee, deposit, targets, approval time, number of partners, fund houses or schemes, AUM, years in business. Placeholders only ("Payout grid to confirm").
- **Features and promises not confirmed**: no partner app, partner desk, back office, CRM, branded app, training, marketing support, "leads we send you", "dedicated relationship manager", until `partner-tools` is answered; no "your clients are always yours" or "we never contact your clients" until `client-ownership` is answered.
- **Mutual fund traps**: never the blocked phrase for commission-less plans, "zero commission", "commission-free"; avoid the phrase "no commission" in any form (it matches the lint even inside "no commission rebates"); no named schemes or fund houses; no "all fund houses"; no "guarantee", "assured", "safe", "risk-free" (also avoid a negated "guarantee").
- **Broking on this page**: no F&O, derivatives, segments or brokerage share (they belong to 63 and would trigger the fo_risk hint); the Authorised Person route is one link and one FAQ answer.
- **Regulatory process numbers**: no NISM exam fees, pass marks, validity or renewal periods, AMFI fees or continuing education hours.
- **Company names and codes (v3)**: no company name in the h1, lead or any h2; the AMFI ARN only as {{reg.wealth.arn}} in the s-apply fine print, the s-agreement card and the disclosure band; Aikyam Capital's SEBI number does not appear on this page.
- **Devices**: no urgency ("limited seats", "join now before"), no "free" or "₹0", no partner testimonials, awards or counts, no regulator logos, no em dashes, no spaced en dashes, no "click here".
- **Judge 12-Oct-2026 additions**: (1) no Aikyam programme rules drafted as partner obligations (brand use in partner ads, exclusivity, targets, termination, cash handling beyond the plain AMFI rule): they belong in the `partner-code` client-text block; the Always and Never lists only restate obligations every distributor already has. (2) No meta lines about the page ("This page shows no earnings examples"): the absence of figures is the compliance, and the line reads as legal copy. (3) No route from ROOT for people without an ARN (an apply button in the "New to distribution?" card, "we help you get your ARN") until `pre-arn-applicants` is answered. (4) No photo art-directed or captioned as a ROOT partner: none exist before launch; a model with a release depicting a distributor's work is fine.

## 1. Job
This page exists so that an active trader or an IPO applicant can see what they can trade on ROOT (shares and ETFs to hold or for the day, IPOs, and, after the risk box, futures and options, currency and commodity), which company provides it, what it costs and where they will place orders, and leaves ready to open a trading and demat account with Aikyam Capital ("Start trading").

- **Primary action** (one, `data-primary`): the hero inline bar (pattern "Hero inline bar form", 01 v3): +91 field and "Start trading" (sitemap `primary_cta`) in one row, the unticked required consent box (09 master draft, `data-draft="consent"`), receiver and hand-off lines as `p.fine`. `form` posts to /open-account/trading, which opens in its OTP-sent state with the number and consent carried over (Home's default, open question 17 in the log). Event `account_start` (entity: capital), `data-form-entity="Aikyam Capital"`. Data receiver: Aikyam Capital Private Limited.
- **Secondary action** (one): "See all charges" to /pricing (s-charges ghost button, s-final outline-light button). Proposed event `view_charges` (section 10). The hero's "See what you pay" is an in-page jump to s-charges, not a second action.
- **Other routes to the same account** (plain links, never `data-primary`): the s-products and s-steps band buttons and the s-final light button ("Start trading"), the header button ({{cta.primary}}, shell) and the mobile bar (build default for this page: Login + "Start trading" to /open-account/trading).
- **Search**: main query "online share trading" (a hub query for product-aware traders; "open demat account online" belongs to 09, the brand query to Home). Supporting queries answered on the page: "intraday vs delivery", "apply for IPO with UPI", "how to activate F&O segment" (answered below the box only, never in the title or description).
  - Title (60 characters): "Online share trading: stocks, ETFs and IPOs | ROOT by Aikyam"
  - Description (145 characters): "Trade shares and ETFs on NSE and BSE, and apply for IPOs, with Aikyam Capital, a SEBI-registered stock broker. See what you pay before you start."
  - Neither names F&O, futures, options, derivatives, currency or commodity: this is an fo_risk page that is not an F&O page (site memory, pilot defaults).

## 5. What this page must not say
- **F&O above the box.** The title, description, eyebrow, h1, lead, form copy, proof points, the hero stage, the s-products h2, rows and explainer cards, and (judge 12-Oct-2026) everything in s-charges and s-steps, which now sit above s-fo, never name F&O, futures, options, derivatives, currency, commodity or CTT. F&O first appears in the s-fo h2, directly above the box.
- **F&O figures or promotion.** No lot sizes, margins, expiry days, leverage, contract values or "trade with margin"; no "multiply", "small capital", "hedge like a pro". No NRML, MIS or CO product codes in the ticket. Current figures are linked to NSE and MCX.
- **Unconfirmed platform claims** (claims.json and the missing feature spec): fast, reliable or instant execution, "zero lag", bank-grade security, advanced charts, TradingView, GTT, basket orders, option chain, API or algo trading (deferred), instant pledge, MTF, "pay later", a watchlist or alerts as a feature. The illustrative screens show only an order ticket, holdings and an IPO application.
- **Prices before the rate card**: no ₹0 account opening, "zero brokerage", "free AMC", "flat ₹X", "per order" pricing (presumes a plan, log row 4), "low brokerage", "no hidden charges", "all charges". "Free" appears only inside the header token {{cta.primary}} (site-level, section 10).
- **App**: no store links, QR, "download now" or "trade on the go"; "coming soon" only; no phone mockup on this page (site memory: Home and Get the App only; conflict logged in section 10).
- **Research and advice**: no tips, stock ideas, "stocks to buy", top gainers, research reports, target prices, recommendations, advisor or adviser.
- **Market data**: no real tickers, company names, ETF names or index levels; example names only (Example Industries Ltd, Example Index ETF); "Illustrative data. Not real prices or returns." directly under any visual with prices.
- **Scale, proof and urgency**: no client, trade or download counts, years, awards, ratings, testimonials, "trusted by", comparisons with any broker; no "open today", cashback, contests or limited periods (incentives for account opening are prohibited for brokers).
- **IPO**: no "in a few taps", pre-apply, "guaranteed" or likely allotment; "Pay with UPI" stays the default line until log rows 2 and 5 are answered.
- **Activation and speed**: no "fully online", "start trading today", "instant activation" or durations; activation is "subject to KYC verification and exchange approval" (plain version allowed in s-steps).
- **Intraday rules from memory**: no square-off times, intraday margin or leverage figures.
- **Safety adjectives**: no "your money is safe", "protected", "segregated" or "secure" beyond the approved hand-off line.
- **Mutual funds**: no "mutual fund", "SIP" or "one account" copy (it would pull in the MF risk line and Q7); this page sells the Aikyam Capital account only.
- **Company names and codes (v3)**: no company name in the h1, lead or any h2; registration codes only in the hero proof points (and the footer strip), never in FAQ answers, tiles or step cards.
- **Wording traps**: no "sub-broker", "IFA", "Direct plans", em dashes, spaced en dashes, "click here"; no "Get OTP" on a link that only jumps within the page.
- **Calculations**: no example trade cost, brokerage calculator output or P&L (rate card unapproved; "No figure computed by Giraffe").

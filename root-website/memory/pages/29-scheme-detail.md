## 1. Job
This page exists so that a first-time investor or goal planner who has found a fund (on the Fund Explorer, the Mutual Funds hub, Google or a friend's link) can see in plain words what the scheme invests in, its NAV, how it has done next to its benchmark, how much risk it carries, what it costs and what Aikyam Wealth earns, and leaves ready to invest in it through Aikyam Wealth Private Limited by starting an investing account with their mobile number ("Invest in this fund").

- **What the page is**: the design template for every scheme page ROOT will generate from the mutual fund data feed (Phase 2, 8 Dec). The scheme, fund house, benchmark, companies and people on it are fictional; every figure is illustrative example data, captioned, and carries a `data-field` name so the developer binds the real value (6.7). Golden for template B: 27, 32, 33, 34, 39 and 40 inherit its layouts (6.8).
- **Primary action** (one, `data-primary`): the submit "Invest in this fund" (sitemap `primary_cta`) in the invest card `section.form-card#s-invest`, nested in the hero's right column. The `form` posts to /open-account/investing (page 10) with the number, the consent and a hidden scheme field; page 10 opens in its OTP-sent state with the number and consent carried over (03's and Home's default; section 10). Event `account_start` (entity wealth), `data-form-entity="Aikyam Wealth"`. Receiver: Aikyam Wealth Private Limited.
- **Secondary action** (one): "Compare flexi cap funds" to `/invest/mutual-funds/explore?category=flexi-cap` (s-returns band link and s-final outline button), proposed event `explore_funds`.
- **Other routes to the same action** (never `data-primary`): "Invest in this fund" jump links to `#s-invest` in s-costs and s-portfolio, the s-final light button, the mobile bar (front matter `"mbar": {"label": "Invest in this fund", "href": "#s-invest"}`, so phones keep the scheme context instead of opening page 10 cold) and the header button (shell; follows the Wealth entity). Judge: no body link to /login (the invest card's login line is cut, section 6.1 row 2).
- **Search**: main query pattern "{scheme name}" (here "example flexi cap fund"); supporting "{scheme} NAV", "{scheme} returns", "{scheme} expense ratio", "{scheme} exit load", "flexi cap fund meaning".
  - Title (62 characters): "Example Flexi Cap Fund: NAV, returns and risk | ROOT by Aikyam". Programmatic rule: "{Scheme name}: NAV, returns and risk | ROOT by Aikyam", dropping " | ROOT by Aikyam" when the result is over 70 characters.
  - Description (158 characters): "See Example Flexi Cap Fund's NAV, returns against its benchmark, riskometer, costs and holdings. Invest through Aikyam Wealth, an AMFI-registered distributor."
  - The example URL itself: noindex and left out of sitemap.xml (a fictional scheme); generated scheme pages are indexable (section 10).

## 3. Users and their anxieties
**U1 New and first-time investors** (primary)
1. "A friend mentioned this fund. Is it too risky for someone like me, and can I lose money?"
2. "How much do I need to start, and what will it cost me?"
3. "Is ROOT showing me this fund because it earns more on it?"

**U4 Goal planners**
1. "Has it really done better than its index over three to five years, or just last year?"
2. "What am I actually buying: which companies, and who runs the money?"
3. "If I need the money early, what do I lose?"

Also reading this page: U7 existing ROOT investors who want to add this fund ("Do I have to sign up again?"); U9 diligence readers (AMFI code for distributors: ARN shown, regular plans only, commission disclosed; SEBI riskometer and performance display).

## 5. What this page must not say
- **Advice, ranking or endorsement** (mandatory question 8; claims.json): "recommended", "best", "top-rated", "handpicked", "curated", "our pick", "funds you should choose", star ratings or third-party ratings, ranks ("Rank 3 in category"), quartiles, "category leader", "consistent performer", "beats its benchmark", "outperformed". The word "rated" is blocked by lint, so the riskometer copy never says "rated Very high" (it says "This fund: Very high"). No peer table on this page (it would rank named schemes); comparison is a link to the Fund Explorer.
- **Return claims or projections**: no "could grow to", SIP projections, "₹10,000 became ₹X", or any figure computed by Giraffe; no annualised figure for periods of one year or less; no return or change figure in the first screen (the hero shows NAV and the chart, no percentage, no day change; chart labels read "High" and "Low", judge); no returns without the benchmark beside them; no "safe", "low risk" (unless the riskometer says Low), "stable", "guaranteed" (blocked), "assured", "capital protection".
- **Real names with illustrative values**: no real scheme, fund house, AMC logo, index (no NIFTY or BSE index names: the benchmark is "Example Broad Market Index (TRI)", extending the site rule that cut the ticker for invented values on real indices), listed company or fund manager. Fictional names only: Example Mutual Fund, Example Flexi Cap Fund, Example Bank Ltd, A. Example. The fund house appears as text in the eyebrow, never as a logo (generated pages too, until section 10 is answered).
- **Plans and commission**: "Direct plan" in any form (blocked; an ARN distributor cannot deal in Direct Plans, so the page never explains "regular versus direct"), "zero commission", "commission-free", "no commission", "free" (review term) for opening the investing account or buying units.
- **Live or speed claims**: "live NAV", "real-time", "instant redemption", "money in your bank in a day", settlement or cut-off times typed from memory (placeholder `nav-cutoff`).
- **Tax**: no rates, holding periods, "tax-free", "save tax" or Section 80C (this is not a tax-saving scheme; 64 Tax Saving owns that).
- **Unconfirmed ROOT facts**: the account charge, transaction charge, SIP availability at launch, SIP features (pause, step-up, date choice), where investors log in (judge: no "Log in to invest in this fund" or any line implying a login opens this fund, and no body link to /login, Phase 3), "track it in the ROOT app" (app coming soon), where units are held ("in your demat"; placeholder `mf-holding` lives on 10).
- **Company names and codes (v3)**: no legal company name in the h1, lead or any h2; the entity sits in the invest card label, the receiver fine print, the costs tile label, the facts card line, FAQ answers, the s-final eyebrow and the disclosure band. "SEBI-registered" never describes Aikyam Wealth (AMFI-registered distributor, ARN). Registration codes appear only as `{{tokens}}`, never typed.
- **Advice words**: "advisor", "adviser", "advice", "experts", "research"; the only exception is the fund house's own product-label footnote if compliance requires it verbatim (section 10).
- **Status by colour alone**: no green or red returns without a sign and a text label; riskometer levels always written out.
- **F&O words**, "one account", "single account", "IFA", "sub-broker", em dashes, spaced en dashes, "click here", urgency ("NFO closing", "invest before the NAV rises").
- **Unlabelled numbers**: every figure sits in a block with a source line and the illustrative line, cards included (judge); a missing value is a dash, never 0% (6.7).
- **Giraffe wording presented as someone else's** (judge): the category lead (`category-line`) and the risk-level line (`risk-level`) are drafts for compliance, never attributed to SEBI or the fund house; only the product label lines are the fund house's words.

### 6.7 Data conventions for template B (proposed site rules; every derivative follows them)
- **Illustrative values, fictional names**: market and fund figures are example data (rules/figma-html.md); never facts about ROOT, Aikyam, a real scheme, a real index or a real company. Facts about ROOT or Aikyam (charges, ARN, platform, contacts) stay `{{tokens}}` or placeholders.
- **`data-field`** on every data value names the vendor field it binds to (`scheme_name`, `amc_name`, `nav`, `nav_date`, `nav_high`, `nav_low`, `return_1y`, `bm_return_1y`, `ter`, `exit_load`, `min_sip`, `min_lumpsum`, `terms_date`, `aum`, `launch_date`, `facts_date`, `benchmark_name`, `riskometer`, `bm_riskometer`, `product_label`, `holdings`, `sectors`, `mcap_split`, `managers`, `sid_url`, `kim_url`, `factsheet_url`, `scheme_code`). Invisible; the developer's binding map.
- **Two lines under every block that shows a number, cards included**: `p.src` (as-on date and source; stays on the live page) then `p.illus` "Illustrative data. Not real prices or returns." (removed at go-live). Dates DD-MMM-YYYY. (Judge: the invest card and the facts card were missing their source lines.)
- **One consistent example set** (judge): every figure agrees with every other. Chart start = NAV / (1 + 1-year return); since-launch CAGR from a ₹10.00 launch NAV over the days to the NAV date; chart axis labels name the range's High and Low, never bare values at the ends. Example set: NAV ₹58.42 (09-Oct-2026); a year ago ₹53.19; 3 years ago ₹37.81; 5 years ago ₹26.51; launch ₹10.00 (14-Jan-2013).
- **Formats**: NAV in ₹ to 2 decimals; returns and expense ratio to 2 decimals; portfolio weights to 1 decimal; CAGR for periods over one year, simple change for one year or less, each labelled; Indian digit grouping (₹12,480 Cr).
- **Missing values**: `<span aria-label="Not available">-</span>`, never 0% (a spaced en dash fails lint).
- **Plans**: regular plans only; Growth shown by default; benchmark returns beside every scheme return; no return or change figure in the hero.
- **Mixed on purpose**: the fund trails its benchmark over 1 and 5 years, so no example reads as a performance claim.
- **Data hero**: the h1 is the item's name at d2 size (`h1.d1.d1--name`); the eyebrow names who stands behind the item; on phones the action card comes before the chart.
